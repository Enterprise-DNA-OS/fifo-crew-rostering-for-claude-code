---
description: Who can fill a gap: everyone in the trade, checked against the site's requirements, the dates, declines, days off and the consecutive shift limit.
---

1. Run `npm run roster -- fill <job> [--trade=<trade>] [--from=<date> --to=<date>]`. With no trade it takes the job's first gap.
2. Present the ready people first. For the rest, say exactly what stops them (a credential, busy days, a licence, too many days in a row).
3. Prefer employees, then people already inducted on the site. Say if someone would be called in on their days off.
4. On the operator's pick, book them with `/assign`.

description: Draft a reminder to a worker whose credential lapses before or during a booked swing.
---

1. Run `npm run roster -- credentials --worker=<who> --json`.
2. Write it to `drafts/credential-<worker>-<code>.md`: what lapses, on what date, the swing it affects, and what to send the office (a photo of the new card or certificate).
3. Never send it.
